How it works

It matches each question to the business’s own answers by keyword, so it never makes things up.

  1. Write the FAQ once

    Hours, prices, bookings, parking: the questions customers ask every day, answered in the owner’s own words.

  2. Customers ask in their own words

    Questions are matched by keyword with typo tolerance. Quick-reply buttons help on phones.

  3. Callbacks come to the owner

    When someone wants a booking or a person, it asks for a name and a phone number or email.

What it does, and what it won’t

Does

  • Only approved answersEvery reply comes from the owner’s list.
  • Works on any siteWordPress, Wix, Squarespace or plain HTML, with one line of code.
  • Takes callback detailsName, phone or email, and a note.
  • Room to growCan later be connected to a free-tier AI model for open-ended questions, if the owner wants that.

Won’t

  • Invent prices, hours or promises.
  • Charge per message or per month.
  • Send anything you type in this demo anywhere.
