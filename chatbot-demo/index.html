Demo businesses: CafePlumberHair salon
This is a demo of a zero-cost website FAQ chatbot. The business is fictional. Click the 💬 button at the bottom right and ask a question (try “are you open sunday?”, “price?”, “book”).
How it works: the chatbot runs entirely in the browser. It matches customer questions to the business’s own FAQ answers (keywords + fuzzy matching), so there are no monthly AI fees. It can collect a name and phone number for callbacks. If you want, it can later be connected to a free-tier AI model for open-ended questions.
Usually answers instantly
